# Brand — CampusGig StacStart Edition

_Status: active_

CampusGig is a trustworthy hiring workspace for verified African university students and the employers ready to judge ability before experience. The StacStart edition centers one idea: practical Proof Tasks make early talent visible.

## Palette

- Ink: near-black navy-green for navigation and high-trust surfaces
- Canvas: cool off-white page background
- Surface: white panels and forms
- Trust green: restrained deep green for primary actions, verification, and successful outcomes
- Amber: pending verification and incomplete proof only
- Red: rejection and destructive actions only

## Typography

- UI: Geist Sans
- Numbers and identifiers: Geist Mono
- Headlines are confident, compact, and sentence case

## Voice

- Direct, useful, and specific
- Serious about opportunity and verification
- Optimistic without startup clichés
- Africa-first through credible names, institutions, companies, and compensation

## Product line

Your first job shouldn’t require your first job.
